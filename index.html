Questions

How does the free trial work?

Sign in with your Google account and use the Advanced plan for 14 days, with no card. Then choose a plan to keep going.

How can I pay?

Credit card, which renews automatically, or Pix, paid per period. Payment goes through the payment provider: FluxGK never sees or stores your card.

Can I cancel?

Anytime. On your first purchase, you get a full refund within 7 days. After that, access continues until the end of the paid period.

Is my data safe?

Each coach sees only their own data. Changing plans or letting a plan expire deletes nothing, and you can delete your account whenever you want.

Which devices does it run on?

In the browser on computer, tablet and phone, and it can be installed as an app.
